Apps

Three apps for Jira Cloud. All three are free and store their data only in Atlassian Forge storage on your own site.

  • Desk Tools MCP

    Five tools that let an AI agent read a Jira Service Management request and write to it. Customer replies stay switched off until a Jira admin turns them on.

    Privacy policy for Desk Tools MCP

  • E-Invoice Kit for Jira

    Creates EN 16931 / XRechnung invoices from the time logged on a Jira issue and checks XML invoice attachments against the rules it supports.

    Privacy policy for E-Invoice Kit for Jira

  • Team Load Radar for Jira

    Shows how much work each person has in progress across all Jira projects and can check a personal limit when work moves to In Progress.

    Privacy policy for Team Load Radar for Jira
